# 古典骨相模块（转译层核心资产）

> 防幼态脸 / 防日韩偶像脸 / 防 AI 网红脸的唯一硬手段：**写骨相，不写美丑形容词**。
> 全肯定句（无 not/no/without），逐字使用可按角色微调措辞，不得改写成近义词堆叠。
> 成年人物头像必选一套融入 Module 1（Character DNA）。

## 一、为什么必须用骨相

- `beautiful / pretty / gorgeous` → 模型落入「网红脸数据集」；
- `large eyes / full lips / gentle` → 幼态化三连（gentle 被读成微笑，一笑苹果肌顶起全脸）；
- 骨相是「结构事实」：三庭、颧骨、下颌、眼型、鼻梁——模型只能按结构画，没有脸型数据集可滑入。

## 二、女性古典骨相（全肯定句 · 已验证）

```text
Her face is built on classical Chinese bone structure. The three horizontal
thirds of her face are close to equal, the mid-face and lower face fully
developed, the chin a proper length. The face is lean, with the cheekbone
and jaw reading clearly beneath the skin. A clean sharp jawline tapers to
a defined chin. Narrow high cheekbones and a subtly defined brow bone give
the face real shadow structure. Her features sit generously far apart,
with open space across the forehead, under the eyes and across the cheeks.
Long narrow almond eyes with a level outer corner and a calm half-lidded
opening. Straight soft sparse brows, a low subtle nose bridge, a straight
fine nose and a small refined tip. A small mouth with a clearly defined
lip peak, a high crown and a smooth full forehead, a long slender neck.
```

## 三、男性古典骨相（全肯定句）

```text
His face is built on classical Chinese bone structure. The three horizontal
thirds are close to equal, the mid-face and lower face fully developed, the
chin a proper length. The face is lean with the cheekbone and jaw reading
clearly beneath the skin, a clean sharp jawline tapering to a defined chin.
Narrow high cheekbones and a subtly defined brow bone give the face real
shadow structure. His features sit generously far apart. Long narrow eyes
with a level outer corner, straight sparse brows, a straight fine nose with
a small refined tip, a mouth of modest size with a clearly defined lip
peak. Weathered skin with natural texture and visible pores, no retouching.
```

## 四、变体适配（按角色卡气质选）

| 角色类型 | 骨相微调方向 |
|---|---|
| 少年郎（14-18） | 保留三庭均等但「the jaw not yet fully developed」；**明写年龄**（He is sixteen years old） |
| 武将 / 力士 | 加厚量感：broad cheekbones, a heavy square jaw, a thick strong neck |
| 老宗师 | 加岁月：deep lines bracket the mouth, the brow heavier with age, the eyes still clear |
| 帝王 / 女帝 | 加威仪：a level unhurried gaze, the brow bone calm and commanding |
| 江湖游侠 | 加风霜：sun-darkened skin, a thin scar through one brow（疤痕=彩蛋位） |

## 五、年龄必须明写（敏感词规避的正路）

不要写「young woman」含糊带过——直接写岁数，成年最安全：

```text
She is twenty-three years old. / He is nineteen years old.
```

## 六、敏感词规避表（正向全文交付前扫查）

高危词根：`baby / teen / child / chubby / doll / pouting / see-through / swimming / wet / nude / bare`

| 想表达 | 别写 | 改写 |
|---|---|---|
| 不要婴儿肥 | no baby fat | the cheekbone and jaw read clearly beneath the skin |
| 不要大圆眼 | not doll eyes | long narrow almond eyes with a level outer corner |
| 不要厚唇 | not pouting | a small mouth with a clearly defined lip peak |
| 湿衣/湿地 | wet clothing / wet earth | the hem darkened where it has touched / damp dark earth |
| 成年女性 | 年龄段含糊 | She is twenty-three years old. |

## 七、防日式漂移锚点词（东方题材必查）

- 中式剑：`a straight double-edged Chinese jian`（别只写 sword）；
- 交领右衽：`cross-collared right over left`（和服相反）；
- 圈椅 / 月洞门 / 盖碗：`a horseshoe-back armchair / a circular moon gate / a lidded gaiwan tea bowl`；
- 木色：`dark red-brown hardwood`（日式是浅杉桧）；
- 避开：`tea room / pale blond wood / minimal / a single branch in a vase`（日式生活方式陷阱）。

## 八、居家感陷阱（古装角色场景选择）

中式服装 + 极简室内 = 无印良品 = 出日本人（类型错误，换词救不回）。安全场景五判据：

1. 户外/半户外「通过性空间」（穿过去，不是坐下来的房间）；
2. 零家具零器物（或只有时代考据过的器物）；
3. 环境高过她、包围她；
4. 画面里有天候（雨/风/雾/雪）；
5. 她在移动或刚停下，是过客不是主人。

（厨房/工坊/医庐等功能性室内空间安全——有劳作痕迹的房间不是「极简室内」。）
